Customer Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of the original purchase.
Refund requests must include the customer ID and payment reference.
Refunds are processed within 5 business days after approval.

Payment Policy
Payments cannot exceed the customer's outstanding balance.
Payments above ₹10,000 require manager approval.
All successful payments must be recorded in the customer ledger.

Customer Support
Customer support is available from 9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.
Urgent payment issues should be escalated to the support manager.
